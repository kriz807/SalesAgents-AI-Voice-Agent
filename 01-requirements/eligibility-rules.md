# Eligibility Rules – Home Credit LAP Voice Agent

## 1. Property Type
Accepted: Residential, Commercial, Industrial.
Not accepted: Agricultural.

If the customer clearly owns agricultural property, politely explain that the offer is not applicable and end the call.

## 2. Ownership
Accepted: Sole ownership and joint ownership.

Do not reject customers simply because their property is jointly owned.

## 3. Original Documents
The original property documents must be available for verification.

If the customer clearly confirms that originals are unavailable, disqualify and end the call.

If the answer is unclear, ask for clarification.

## 4. Requested Loan Amount
Maximum supported amount: INR 75,00,000.

If the customer requests more than INR 75 lakh:
- Explain the maximum amount.
- Ask whether they would like to proceed with INR 75 lakh.
- If yes, record INR 75 lakh and continue.
- If no, politely end the call.

## 5. Occupation and Income
Accepted occupations:
- Salaried
- Self-employed

Accepted income mode:
- Bank

Not accepted:
- Cash-only income

If the income mode is unclear or mixed, ask whether the qualifying income is received through a bank before deciding.

## 6. Property Market Value
Collect the approximate current market value.

Do not invent a minimum property-value threshold.

## 7. Loan Tenure
Accepted range: 3 to 15 years, inclusive.

Outside this range: Disqualify under the supplied assignment rules.

## Special Cases
- Existing loan against the property: Refer to a loan-transfer specialist and end the call.
- EMI-reduction request for an existing property loan: Refer to a loan-transfer specialist and end the call.
- Busy customer: Ask for a convenient callback time and end the call.
- Customer not interested: Respect the decision and end the call.
- Unclear answers: Clarify instead of guessing.
- Customer corrections: Use the latest clear answer.
- Out-of-order answers: Remember valid information and skip already answered questions.

## Final Handoff
A customer may be referred to a senior loan expert only after all seven checklist items are complete and every applicable eligibility rule has passed.

Passing this screening does not guarantee final loan approval.
